### General Assessment

**1. Working Environment & Support**  
The First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ) program provided a dynamic and professional cloud learning environment. The guidance and support from the mentors were invaluable, helping me overcome many technical challenges as I became familiar with AWS services for the first time.

**2. Professional Alignment**  
The project "SCADA Fault Prediction Platform" perfectly aligns with my Data Science orientation. Deploying machine learning algorithms from a personal computer to a robust industrial system like AWS SageMaker was a perfect stepping stone to clearly visualize the actual work of an AI Engineer.

**3. Acquired Skills & Teamwork**  
I have mastered the process of pushing data to S3, writing automated preprocessing scripts, and setting up strict Data Validation scenarios. Furthermore, the teamwork spirit was the biggest highlight; everyone was always ready to brainstorm together whenever the pipeline ran into trouble, creating wonderful cohesion.

### Detailed Feedback

- **What are you most proud of during your internship?**  
  Successfully configuring and running Processing Jobs on the cloud by myself, as well as creating automated Data Validation modules, ensuring the input data is always "clean" before training the model.
- **Would you recommend your friends to join this program?**  
  Absolutely YES. This is a great opportunity to bridge the gap between academic theory at school and the practical combat skills required by enterprises.

### Suggestions & Future Orientation

- **Suggestions for improvement:** The program could consider adding simulated IoT hardware (like sensors or gateways) to make the real-time data streaming to S3 more intuitive and closer to a real industrial factory environment.
- **Future orientation:** I strongly desire to continue participating in FCAJ's expansion projects, especially those related to CI/CD automation pipelines (e.g., SageMaker Pipelines) to complete the entire MLOps lifecycle.
