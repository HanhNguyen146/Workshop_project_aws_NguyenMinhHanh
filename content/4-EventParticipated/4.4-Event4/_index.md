# Reflection on “Agentic AI Build Week & Hackathon Journey”

### Purpose of the Event

- Experience a continuous 24-hour cycle of ideating and perfecting Agentic AI products.
- Listen to outstanding practical AI solutions from competing teams.
- Develop resilience to overcome technical issues and manage time.

### List of Speakers

- Mr. Joseph Marazota (Head of Technology for ASEAN, AWS)
- Hackathon Teams (One Team, Signal Scout, Team Plan, 3K, Six Pillar)

---

### Key Highlights

#### 1. Opening Speech (Joseph Marazota)

- Vision of AI shaping industries and the critical role of humans (human in the loop).

#### 2. KFC Food Ordering Project (1st Place - One Team)

- Zalo AI assistant using Tiny Fish and Agent Core to remember conversations, with ultra-low latency and optimized costs.

#### 3. Strategy Analysis Project (2nd Place - Signal Scout)

- Gathering competitor signals, using multiple Subagents, Bedrock Guardrail, and LlamaIndex to avoid AI hallucinations.

#### 4. Solution Architect Assistant (Team Plan)

- Automatically drawing Draw.io diagrams and generating Terraform code from natural language requirements.

#### 5. Crowd Management (Team 3K - Sheper) & Anti-Money Laundering (Six Pillar)

- Coordinating Kinesis, YOLO, AgentCore for video stream processing; Automated fraud analysis system across 3 layers (XGBoost, Step Functions, Agent).

---

### Results Achieved

- Mastered how to combine Agentic AI models with AWS cloud infrastructure.
- Understood the "Done is better than perfect" philosophy in Hackathons.

---

### Applications to Work

- Apply Agentic AI in personal projects to automate action chains.
- Better manage project scope in future teamwork.

---

### Experience in the Event

- Exploded with emotions, debugging overnight until morning and bursting with joy upon successful demo.
