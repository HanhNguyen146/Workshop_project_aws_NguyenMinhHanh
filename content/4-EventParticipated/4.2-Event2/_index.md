# Reflection on “DevOps Reality & URL Shortener Architecture”

### Purpose of the Event
- Discover the real work of DevOps and Data Analytics engineers.
- Learn how to design highly scalable systems on AWS.
- Understand personal development roadmaps and culture at multinational corporations (MNCs).

### List of Speakers
- Mr. Trong H. Truong
- Mr. Dinh Trung Kien & Nguyen Minh Tho
- Mr. Danh Hoang Hieu Nghi
- Mr. Dat Pham & Cuong Nguyen

---

### Key Highlights
#### 1. The Reality of a DevOps Engineer
- Breaking the myth that DevOps is only CI/CD or Docker. In reality, it involves troubleshooting and on-call duties. Systems thinking and automation are paramount.
#### 2. URL Shortener Service Architecture
- System design analysis using Amazon CloudFront, AWS WAF, ECS (running Key Generation Service), ElastiCache Redis, and DynamoDB. Emphasizing edge defense and read/write separation.
#### 3. From First Cloud AI Journey to AWS Partner
- An 8-step career roadmap from student to AWS Partner, the importance of building a portfolio and giving back to the community.
#### 4. Real Stories & MNC Culture
- Data Engineer work at Kamereo and Colgate. The ability to do "Data Storytelling". The 5-stage development roadmap and No-Blame culture.

---

### Results Achieved
- Better understand the reality of Cloud/DevOps jobs.
- Master the principles of designing high-load systems and cache-aside patterns.

---

### Applications to Work
- Apply static/dynamic processing separation architecture in school projects.
- Practice Root Cause Analysis skills.

---

### Experience in the Event
- The event provided very authentic perspectives, helping me avoid "illusions" about the IT industry's glamour.
