During my internship, I had the opportunity to attend a series of four events organized by the AWS community. These events provided me with practical knowledge in Cloud Computing, Security, DevOps, and Agentic AI, as well as valuable real-world skills and experiences. By listening to insights from industry experts, observing competitions from outstanding teams, and networking with professionals, I expanded my perspective on career development pathways while solidifying my technical foundation, learning system architecture design, and maintaining a continuous learning mindset.

---

### [Event 1](4.1-Event1/)
**Event Name:** AWS Community Meetup

**Time:** 09:00 AM, June 06, 2026

**Location:** 26th Floor, Bitexco Financial Tower, 02 Hai Trieu Street, Sai Gon Ward, Ho Chi Minh City

**Role:** Attendee

**Description:** The meetup covered a wide range of topics from deep technical aspects to soft skills, including: Applying Machine Learning to NIDS systems combined with AWS WAF; Docker technology; the journey from IT Helpdesk to Senior Sysadmin; designing Multiplayer games with Godot and WebSockets; the art of teamwork; and the application of GraphRAG.

**Key Takeaways:** Through the event, I grasped the latest technology trends (security, containers, AI) and honed effective teamwork skills, consolidating my personal career development orientation.

### [Event 2](4.2-Event2/)
**Event Name:** DevOps Reality & URL Shortener Architecture

**Time:** 09:00 AM, June 13, 2026

**Location:** 26th Floor, Bitexco Financial Tower, 02 Hai Trieu Street, Sai Gon Ward, Ho Chi Minh City

**Role:** Attendee

**Description:** The event delved into the reality of a DevOps Engineer's work, debunking many common myths. In addition, experts shared the standard architecture for a URL shortener on AWS, an 8-step roadmap for career development with Cloud, and real stories about data analytics at MNCs.
