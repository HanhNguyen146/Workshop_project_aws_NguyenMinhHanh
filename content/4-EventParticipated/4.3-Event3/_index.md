# Summary Report: FCAJ-Agentic AI BUILD WEEK

## Overview

As part of the internship program, I attended a technical sharing session where several student teams presented their projects developed during different Hackathon competitions.

The speakers introduced their project ideas, explained how they identified real-world problems, designed technical solutions, and implemented their systems within a limited development time. Besides presenting the technical aspects, they also shared valuable experiences about teamwork, project management, problem-solving strategies, and presentation skills during the competitions.

The event provided practical insights into the complete software development process, from idea generation to final product demonstration, while highlighting the importance of collaboration and continuous learning.

---

## Main Topics

During the sharing session, the presenters discussed several topics, including:

- Introduction to Hackathon competitions and their objectives.
- Project ideation and problem identification.
- System architecture and technology stack selection.
- Challenges encountered during development.
- Team collaboration and task management.
- Demonstration of completed projects.
- Lessons learned and recommendations for future participants.

---

## Key Takeaways

After attending the session, I gained several valuable insights:

- Understanding how to transform an initial idea into a working prototype within a limited timeframe.
- Appreciating the importance of effective teamwork and communication throughout software development.
- Learning practical approaches to selecting suitable technologies based on project requirements.
- Recognizing the necessity of time management and task prioritization during intensive development cycles.
- Becoming more confident in presenting technical projects and explaining design decisions.
- Gaining motivation to participate in future Hackathon competitions to improve both technical and soft skills.
